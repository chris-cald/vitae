# Feature: Gap and Opportunity Analysis

Identify missing or weakly matched skills/requirements and suggest transferable/adjacent skills using semantic similarity.

## Story: Gap and Opportunity Analysis

### Task: Identify missing or weakly matched skills/requirements
- Use embedding similarity and rule-based checks
- Flag gaps and low-similarity areas in feedback

### Task: Suggest transferable/adjacent skills
- Leverage semantic closeness to recommend related skills
- Provide rationale for each suggestion
